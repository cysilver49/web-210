Our Story

For over 20 years, Luigi's has brought the rich flavors of Italy to your table. We use only the freshest ingredients and traditional recipes passed down through generations.

Bill Of Fare

Margherita Pizza

A Neapolitan pie topped with tomato sauce, fresh mozzarella cheese, and basil.

Spaghetti Carbonara

Pasta tossed with a silky sauce made from egg yolks, cured pork cheek (guanciale), and pecorino or parmesan cheese.

Tiramisu

Delicate ladyfingers dipped in espresso and layered with a whipped mixture of eggs, sugar, and mascarpone cheese.

Gelato

A denser, richer Italian-style ice cream available in a wide array of fruit and nut flavors.
